Flagship innovation

CountrySuite

One institutional platform for public services, regulation, workflows and national registries. CountrySuite brings government workspaces and citizen access onto a shared, extensible foundation.

Make services easier to find and use

CountrySuite brings schools, hospitals, courts, revenue offices and other registered public services into citizen-facing directories and guided service journeys.

  • Discover services by country, sector and location
  • Route people to specialised applications
  • Keep public information connected to institutional records

Give every institution the right workspace

Role-based access resolves one sign-in into the workspace a country administrator, organisation, management unit or citizen needs.

  • Controlled country and organisation access
  • Configurable dashboards and permissions
  • Traceable approvals and official correspondence

Build once on shared national foundations

Accounts, geography, workflow and internal mail form a reusable core, allowing new sector modules to extend the platform without becoming isolated systems.

  • Administrative boundaries and PostGIS geography
  • Entitlement-driven module access
  • Reusable workflow and communication services
